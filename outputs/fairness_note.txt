1. Automated scores may reflect biased patterns in historical hiring data and can disadvantage underrepresented groups.
2. Resume wording, career gaps, education access and language differences can affect scores even when job ability is similar.
3. A score is only a decision-support signal; it must not be the sole reason to reject or shortlist a candidate.
4. A human reviewer should check evidence against job-related criteria, investigate anomalies, provide accommodations and allow candidates to correct errors.
